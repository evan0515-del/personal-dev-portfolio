## Problem

The existing tooling split campaign management, content editing, and catalog maintenance into disconnected surfaces. Teams could ship, but coordination cost was high and simple launch workflows involved too much context switching.

## Solution

Commerce Studio consolidated those workflows into a single operational product with shared primitives:

- reusable editorial forms
- preview-friendly content blocks
- campaign status controls
- catalog health indicators

## Implementation notes

The system was intentionally built around reusable screen patterns rather than one-off admin pages. That choice made the codebase less glamorous at the start, but far easier to scale once the number of tools expanded.

## Outcome

The biggest win was not visual polish. It was reducing operational drag. Marketers, merchandisers, and developers could work from the same source of truth and launch sequences became easier to reason about.

## Extension ideas

This is a natural candidate for future experiments around personalization, recommendation APIs, and AI-assisted merchandising workflows.
